FAQ

Good to know.

Do rental hours include setup and breakdown?

No. Your booked hours are for full photo booth service. We set up before your scheduled start time and break down afterward.

Do you offer prints?

VELO currently specializes in a digital iPad photo booth experience with instant text and email sharing.

How far do you travel?

Complimentary travel is included within 25 miles of Valparaiso, Indiana. Additional fees may apply beyond this radius.

Can the photo design match my event?

Yes. Your photo overlay and welcome screen can be customized with names, dates, colors, logos, or event branding.

Can I add more time?

Yes. Additional booth time can be added based on availability.
